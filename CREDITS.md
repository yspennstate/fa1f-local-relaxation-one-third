# Credits

The two conjectures are stated in F. Martinelli, A. Shapira and C. Toninelli, *Long time behaviour of one facilitated
kinetically constrained models: results and open problems*, arXiv:2510.20461. The model is due to G. H. Fredrickson and
H. C. Andersen, Phys. Rev. Lett. 53 (1984).

The paper rests on the following work.

- O. Blondel, N. Cancrini, F. Martinelli, C. Roberto and C. Toninelli, *Fredrickson-Andersen one spin facilitated model
  out of equilibrium*, Markov Process. Related Fields 19 (2013): exponential convergence for q > 1/2 and the block
  argument used in Section 4.
- N. Cancrini, F. Martinelli, C. Roberto and C. Toninelli, *Kinetically constrained spin models*, Probab. Theory Related
  Fields 140 (2008), and *Facilitated spin models: recent and new results* (2009): the spectral gap bounds.
- T. Mountford and G. Valle, *Exponential convergence for the Fredrickson-Andersen one-spin facilitated model*,
  J. Theoret. Probab. 32 (2019): the case of a single initial infection.
- N. Cancrini, F. Martinelli, R. Schonmann and C. Toninelli, J. Stat. Phys. 138 (2010); P. Diaconis and L. Saloff-Coste,
  Ann. Appl. Probab. 6 (1996); A. Ertul, arXiv:2103.00019; M. Gluchowski and G. Menz, arXiv:2611.17861.

The full list of references is in the paper.
